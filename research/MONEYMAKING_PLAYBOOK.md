## §1. Skills income — data analytics (Romania-based)

All data pulled 2026-07-12.

### 1.1 What the employment route actually pays (real salary data)

- **Paylab.ro** (survey, **n=109 verified respondents**, all seniority levels):
  80% of Romanian data analysts earn **4,393–11,868 RON net/month**
  (~**€865–2,340** at ~5.07 RON/EUR). Bottom decile <4,393 RON; gross range
  4,050–19,552 RON/mo.
  Source: https://www.paylab.ro/en/salaryinfo/economy-finance-accountancy/data-analyst
- **PayScale**: entry-level (1–3 yrs) average **110,031 RON/year** gross
  ≈ 9,170 RON/mo gross ≈ **~5,400 RON net/mo (~€1,060)** at the standard
  ~41.5% employee wedge (25% CAS + 10% CASS + 10% tax on remainder).
  Source: https://www.payscale.com/research/RO/Job=Data_Analyst/Salary
- **Glassdoor's junior-Bucharest page is garbage** (unit errors: "RON 2,825/year";
  n=20 self-reports) — discarded, noted here so it isn't re-pulled later.

**Honest junior figure: ~4,000–5,500 RON net/mo (~€790–1,085) for year 1.**
This is a *career income replacement/addition*, not passive yield — 40 h/wk.

### 1.2 Is anyone actually hiring juniors? (real listings, July 2026)

- **Glassdoor**: 657 "data analyst" jobs in Romania (July 2026).
- **LinkedIn**: "data analyst" Romania shows "5,000+" but junior-specific counts
  collapse to **268** ("Data Analyst Junior") or even **3** ("Junior Data
  Analyst") — headline counts are keyword-inflated noise; the junior-accessible
  market is 1–2 orders of magnitude smaller than headlines.
- **juniors.ro** (dedicated Romanian junior-IT board, fetched live): **6 recent
  junior-track data listings**, including **Junior Data Analyst @ Deloitte
  (București, posted 3 days ago)**, Junior Power BI Developer @ Société Générale,
  Data Analyst @ ING Bank, Data Analyst @ Xebia (remote). Postings 2 days–2 weeks
  old. No salaries disclosed.
  Source: https://juniors.ro/jobs/data-science

**Read:** demand at the junior level is real but thin — a handful of live
openings at big-name employers at any given moment, concentrated in Bucharest,
with one remote. This supports "apply now, expect competition," not "easy entry."

### 1.3 Remote-EU junior route (weak)

**Remotive** (data category, fetched live): visible listings are senior- and
US/Canada-skewed; **zero junior EU-open data roles in the visible sample**.
Remote junior data work from Romania via international boards is the *hard*
version of this path, not the default. Local/hybrid Bucharest listings dominate.
(Caveat: free tier shows a small slice of the database — this is a signal, not a
census.)

### 1.4 Freelance route (Upwork et al.) — rates real, cold-start unproven

- **Rates** (Upwork's own published cost pages, via search — direct page 403s):
  entry data analysts ~**$20/hr**, median ~$30/hr, expert $50+.
- **Fees**: since May 2025 Upwork charges a **variable 0–15% per-contract fee**
  (algorithmic; ~10% typical), replacing the old flat 10%. Plus paid "connects"
  to bid (~$0.15 ea, typical bid 8–16 connects).
  Source: https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538
- **The number that matters is missing:** there is **no official or otherwise
  verifiable base rate for what fraction of new freelancer profiles ever land a
  first contract.** Searched for it directly; Upwork does not publish it and
  third-party figures are blog claims. Every freelance income anecdote is
  survivorship-biased and this run cannot correct for it — hence **NOT
  VALIDATED** for the cold-start, and p25 = €0.
- Arithmetic *if* clients land: 10 h/wk × $20/hr × ~90% post-fee × ~0.70
  post-PFA-tax ≈ **$540/mo ≈ €480 net** — treat as p75 conditional on demand
  materializing, not expected value.

### 1.5 Legality / taxes (Romania, 2026 — verified against multiple RO tax guides)

- Freelance income requires a **PFA** (or SRL): 10% income tax on net income
  + **10% CASS** (health; base floored at 6 min-salaries when income exceeds
  that threshold, capped at 72) + **25% CAS** (pension) mandatory only above
  **48,600 RON/yr net** (12 × 4,050 RON min gross salary as of Jan 2026).
  All-in burden on a moderate freelance income ≈ **30–35%**.
  Sources: contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026, regnet.ro/calculator-taxe-pfa-2026
- PFA registration: ~€100 all-in, ~1–2 weeks, fully legal for cross-border
  services within EU. Employment route needs nothing.

### 1.6 Verdict

**VIABLE (employment route)** — the single best-evidenced direction available:
real openings this week, real salary data (n=109), zero startup capital, and it
compounds (junior → mid pay roughly doubles per the 80% band). Costs: 40 h/wk
and 2–6 months of application grind with rejection risk; the p25 outcome at the
6-month mark is *still not hired*. The freelance sub-route is **NOT VALIDATED**:
real rates, unverifiable cold-start odds — usable as a parallel lottery ticket
while studying, not as a plan.

**What closes the gap fastest (per the objective's question):** portfolio
assets that map to the listings actually seen — SQL + Power BI/Tableau dashboards
and an end-to-end analysis writeup (the ING/Deloitte/SocGén listings are
BI-and-reporting-shaped, not ML-shaped). Deep-dive build reserved for Phase 2
if this direction stays top-ranked.
